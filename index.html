Why Only 3 Cabins — The Full Strategic Logic
This is the most important decision in the entire project. Four separate forces all point to the same answer: 3 cabins is the optimal number. Not a limitation — a precision strategy for maximum return.
Reason 01
Septic Is the Hidden Ceiling
Wayne County is entirely off municipal sewer. Under 1,000 gal/day = residential approval — fast and affordable. Over 1,000 gal/day = PA DEP oversight + $200,000+ in commercial infrastructure. 3 cabins + lodge = est. 600–800 gal/day — safely below the threshold. Build 10 cabins and you cross into commercial territory.
Reason 02
Market Psychology & ADR Protection
NYC and Philadelphia guests pay $380–$429/night to feel they have 25 private acres entirely to themselves. 3 cabins = private estate experience ($380+/night). 10 cabins = dense resort ($200/night). 20 cabins = budget campground ($150/night). 3 × $380 beats 10 × $200 every time. Scarcity multiplies profit per unit.
Reason 03
Permitting Speed & Cost
Damascus Township: multiple structures on one parcel = Land Development classification. 3 cabins handled as phased residential additions = faster approval, lower legal cost. 10+ cabins trigger Major Land Development review = adds 12–24 months and $100,000+ in engineering fees. We enter the market faster and cheaper.
Reason 04
Phase 2 Optionality Preserved
3 cabins in Phase 1 leaves 20+ acres completely unencumbered. 12 months of real operating data tells us exactly what Phase 2 should be. Phase 2 investors pay a premium to join a proven operating asset. Negotiating position is dramatically stronger with evidence than with projections.
SMART MOVE 3 cabins is the mathematically optimal choice — highest ROI per dollar, fastest permitting, lowest complexity, maximum land value protection of any build scenario on this 25-acre site.

Perk Test & SEO — The #1 Action Before Anything Else
Before you sign a purchase agreement, hire an architect, or show this report to a single investor — one thing must happen first. The Perk Test.

If the perk test fails completely, you cannot build a single cabin regardless of how much money you have. No septic permit = no building permit = no Certificate of Occupancy = cannot legally operate. This test costs $500–$1,500 and takes 2–4 weeks to book. It must happen before closing.

Who Is the SEO?
SEO = Sewage Enforcement Officer — a PA state-licensed official. Has NOTHING to do with Google search engine optimization.
The SEO's only job: come to your land, dig test holes, time water absorption, issue official approval or rejection for your septic permit.
ONLY a licensed SEO can legally conduct this test and issue the permit. No contractor, architect, or engineer can substitute.
Cost: $500–$1,500 | Lead time: 2–4 weeks minimum. Book immediately upon going under contract — do not wait.
Wayne County Soil — The 3 Possible Outcomes
Soil ConditionWhat HappensExtra CostSignal
Sandy loam — good drainageStandard conventional septic approved — proceed with full build plan as designed$0 extra✓ GO
Clay or shale-heavy — poor drainage (common in Wayne County glacial till)Raised Mound System required — drain field built above ground in imported clean sand$45,000 — already budgeted in CAPEX⚠ CAUTION
Wetland conflict or complete absorption failurePA DEP variance required — major site redesign or walk away from purchase$50,000+ and 6–18 months added✗ STOP

Why Wayne County is not routine: Wayne County sits on glacial till — a mixture of clay, shale, and rock deposited by ancient glaciers. This soil type often drains poorly. The perk test result is unpredictable even on a small area of the same parcel. This is why the $45,000 raised mound system is budgeted as a fixed CAPEX item, not a contingency — it is a real possibility, not a remote risk.
